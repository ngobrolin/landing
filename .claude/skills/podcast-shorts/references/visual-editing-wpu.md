## What v2 lacks

1. **Frame occupancy.** V2 uses substantial navy margins, especially around the stacked duo panes. WPU's people or explanatory material generally own the portrait frame. Design for the phone canvas before applying UI-safe text margins; the safe rectangle is not a maximum picture size.
2. **Hierarchy linked to meaning.** V2 treats every caption similarly. WPU alternates small spoken captions with deliberate large idea words and short hook phrases. Propose restrained emphasis for “LLM”, “kuda liar”, and “kendali”, rather than bouncing every word.
3. **Visual explanation.** V2 illustrates the entire analogy only with host footage. Add a few intentional explanatory cutaways: an appropriately licensed horse visual or an authored conceptual diagram showing model, constraints and direction. Do not fabricate a quote or imply a visual appeared in the source podcast.
4. **Editorial variety.** V2 changes pane geometry but repeats the same information. Switch between a speaker, a reaction, and an explanatory insert when the meaning changes, rather than scheduling movement for its own sake.
5. **Integrated captions.** Captions currently sit detached below the picture. Bring them into the lower-third composition, with a local backing only when needed for contrast. Preserve faces, source credits, original wording and platform-safe placement.
6. **Specific continuation.** The generic domain outro gives little indication of what comes next. A compact real episode thumbnail plus title and “Tonton obrolan lengkap” can provide a clearer destination. Use real episode assets, not invented metadata or subscriber claims.

## Proposed next direction

Keep the Ngobrolin identity. Prefer a cold open on the actual hook with a small logo bug, larger speaker composition, short phrase captions, selective orange emphasis and a few semantic cutaways. End on a concise episode-specific continuation card. Avoid copying WPU's neon set, heavy flash/meme effects, or visual identity.

WPU's inspected examples are purpose-shot direct-to-camera material with clean source imagery. The current podcast composite has burned-in live chat and limited clean crop space. A clean per-host recording would improve the result more than additional border decoration. When only the composite exists, use wider source shots and explanatory cutaways rather than cropping mouths or pretending the overlay can be cleanly removed.
